The best fusion model is \acs{CatBoost} with 3{,}000 iterations, learning rate 0.0118, depth 10, and \(\ell_2\) leaf regularization 1.005, selected by Optuna. We compute \acs{SHAP} values to interpret the fitted model. As shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:shap_summary}, \acs{MS-SSIM} and \acs{UQI} contribute most to the fused prediction, while \acs{VIF}, \acs{PSNR-B}, \acs{SNR}, and \acs{PSNR} have moderate effects.

Our \acs{NR}-\acs{IQA} model, pMOS-Face, uses pseudo-\acs{MOS} from the \acs{FR} fusion and evaluates on faces with human labels. Fig.~\ref{fig:nr-iqa-metric} shows two scenarios. On training data with pseudo and ground-truth \acs{MOS} we obtain \acs{PLCC} 0.9804, \acs{SRCC} 0.9770, \acs{MSE} 6.165, \acs{MAE} 1.638. On the disjoint test set we obtain \acs{PLCC} 0.9679, \acs{SRCC} 0.9691, \acs{MSE} 35.34, \acs{MAE} 4.743, confirming generalization to unseen human labels and alignment with the pseudo supervision.
